# WoW Forever facts

Game and API facts the addon's code depends on, so each session doesn't have to research them again. **Facts only:** each one gives its source, or the issue where it was researched and sourced. The launch-day checks (Nov 4) were completed in game and worked (#197), except the four IDs below that nobody could reach and the Salvation, Mass Salvation and Revoke checks (Blizzard removed those spells, #208): the facts that worked are marked **confirmed in game**, with their sources kept. A fact nobody has confirmed goes on a new issue labelled `quality`, with its source and how to check it in game, and is marked here with a link to that issue (four map and instance IDs the owner can't reach yet are on #207). Three facts that #50's list didn't name were settled by the owner on Nov 8 (#197): the `/say` rule, the spell IDs and the mana classes, each with the owner as its source below. Blizzard removed Salvation, Mass Salvation and Revoke right before launch (owner, 2026-11-08); their lines below keep their beta sources, and the code is #208's.

WoW Forever isn't Classic Era (AGENTS.md's "Issues and specs"). Check new facts against Forever sources (Wowhead's `/forever/` pages, patch notes), put the sources on the issue, and add the fact here in the same PR. When an unconfirmed fact is settled in game, mark its line confirmed here. Where this file and CONTRIBUTING.md differ, CONTRIBUTING.md wins and this file is fixed.

Many sessions couldn't reach wowhead.com or warcraft.wiki.gg and used web search instead; the issues say which.

## The client

- Forever is client 1.60.1, interface `16001` (the TOC's `## Interface`), with the retail UI and the addon compartment. Source: #93 ([TOC format](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/TOC_format); Forever addons on CurseForge such as [Forever LazyChatButtons](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/forever-lazychatbuttons)). Whether the compartment is there: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).
- It uses the modern (12.x) API, with Midnight's addon restrictions. Source: [#32](https://github.com/CKozanecki/FriendlyForever/issues/32#issuecomment-6484071238) ([classicwowforever.com](https://classicwowforever.com/guides/wow-forever-addons-api-compatibility/), [Patch 12.0.0 planned API changes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_12.0.0/Planned_API_changes)); community sources, no blue post.
- Addons ship their own icons as TGA through `## IconTexture`; the game loads TGA and BLP reliably, not PNG. Source: #65, #93. How the icon looks: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).

## Secret values

- In restricted moments the game hands addons **secret values**. Addon code must never compare, index, measure, concatenate or send one; it tests for them with `issecretvalue` and `issecrettable` (`Util.IsSecret`, `Util.Readable`). Source: #32 (Midnight's rules, sources above); AGENTS.md's "Secret values".
- Chat received inside an instance can arrive as secret values. Source: #32. Whether it does: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).
- Whether your own PvP flag, and other players' flags and names, are secret, and when: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4). The addon reads them as possibly secret. Source: as built for #27 and #30 (`Offers.lua`, `GroupBuffs.lua`).
- Whether group members' roles and aura casters are readable: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4) (from #68).

How the simulated client models secrets, and its limits, is in [simulated-client.md](simulated-client.md).

## Chat

- A chat message is at most 255 bytes (`Util.MAX_CHAT_BYTES`), and may not contain `|` escape codes. Source: as built since #20; the simulated client enforces both.
- Addons may use `/say` outdoors only right after a key press (a hardware event); inside instances they may use it freely. Source: [Patch 8.2.5 API changes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_8.2.5/API_changes); as built (README's Buff thanks, `ChatQueue.lua`). Confirmed in game: the owner tested it before the beta ended (Nov 8).
- Whether text emotes (`/thank`) also need a key press: the addon assumes they do. Source: #52 (PR #20). **Confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).
- Whether `C_ChatInfo.PerformEmote("THANK", unit)` (with `DoEmote` as the fallback) is aimed at that unit. Source: #52. **Confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).
- The game has a chat lockdown (`C_ChatInfo.InChatMessagingLockdown`); nothing is sent while it's on. Source: #32. Whether it covers addon messages: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).
- Raid join messages do appear on Forever: the maintainer saw them on the beta, 2026-10-22. Source: #15.

## Addon messages

From #32 ([research comment](https://github.com/CKozanecki/FriendlyForever/issues/32#issuecomment-6484071238); [SendAddonMessage](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_ChatInfo.SendAddonMessage), [Patch 10.2.7 API changes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_10.2.7/API_changes), [Icy Veins: combat restrictions eased](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/combat-addon-restrictions-eased-in-midnight/)). Every item here is **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4) (the #32 checks).

- Since 10.2.7, `C_ChatInfo.SendAddonMessage` is throttled per prefix: 10 messages, refilled at 1 a second. The server can change these numbers at any time (#32).
- A throttled send returns `Enum.SendAddonMessageResult.AddonMessageThrottle`; 0 means success (#32).
- A prefix is at most 16 characters, and a message at most 255 bytes (#32).
- Under Midnight's rules addons may not message other players inside instances (on live: boss encounters and Mythic+) (#32).
- Guild addon messages come back to their sender, and every guild member, the sender included, gets the guild's addon messages in the same order: the order the server relays them. A message is relayed to everyone or lost for everyone. Guild hellos' cap (#38) relies on all three: a copy greets only once its own `GREET` is back with fewer than K others before it. Until one of its own has come back in a session (the game might not send them back, or might spell our name differently), a `GREET` that never comes back still greets, after the random wait, so this can't silence every hello. Source: #32 (they come back), #38 (the order). **Confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).
- Message types so far: `HELLO` (#32); `ANSWER` (#37), whispered to a player whose favour you just returned, so their copy doesn't owe you one back; `PLAN` (#36), whispered to a player you're exchanging buffs with, naming the buff your button holds for them; `RECEIPT` (#138), whispered to a player outside your group your buff just landed on, so their copy can name you when their game can't; and `GREET` (#38), sent to the guild by a copy about to greet a guildmate who logged in, with the line it picked. Whether an addon whisper reaches a stranger nearby decides whether `ANSWER` and `PLAN` work at all; without them, buff back still stops after one round within its 10-minute window (#37), and a thank-you waits only up to its 5-second cap (#36). Confirmed in game (launch, Nov 4) (#37, #36).
- The sender name's form in `CHAT_MSG_ADDON` (`First-Surname` or `First Surname`), and whether an addon whisper reaches a stranger nearby (#32). Receipts depend on both, and on whether a stranger's aura caster really is unreadable, which decides how much they help (#138). Confirmed in game (launch, Nov 4) (#138).
- Receipts are never sent inside any instance: the addon reads `IsInInstance()` and counts an unreadable answer as inside, the pessimistic reading of Midnight's rule above (#138).

## Names and races

- Every character has a first name and a surname (`Nikki-Shifts`), with no realms, and the game can't find a player by first name alone. Source: [Icy Veins: names in Forever](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/names-are-different-in-wow-forever-here-are-the-rules-and-the-official-look/); as built (README, `Util.lua`). How `/targetexact` spells a two-part name, and how `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SENT` gives the target: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).
- Until a group member's details load (just after they join), the client can name them `UNKNOWNOBJECT` ("Unknown" in English), and the addon treats that as no readable name (`Util.UnitFullName`). Source: #99, found by the UI simulator (#83, PR #96); the default UI's `UNKNOWNOBJECT` global string. What Forever's `UnitName` returns then (with or without a surname, secret or not), and whether `UNIT_NAME_UPDATE` fires when it loads: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).
- There are 10 playable races: Classic's eight plus the two Skyborne peoples, High Order (Alliance) and Windshaper (Horde). Source: [#52](https://github.com/CKozanecki/FriendlyForever/issues/52#issuecomment-6484052636) ([Warcraft Tavern: races](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/races/), [seemeta](https://seemeta.com/en/wow-forever/races)).
- The Skyborne share one race file name; the addon tells them apart by race ID 95 (High Order) and 96 (Windshaper), with the faction as the fallback. Source: #52 (no external source for the IDs). The race and ID in `/friendly status`: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).

## Maps and capitals

From #34 ([research comment](https://github.com/CKozanecki/FriendlyForever/issues/34#issuecomment-6484024537); [C_Map.GetBestMapForUnit](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_Map.GetBestMapForUnit), [Warcraft Tavern: Skyborne](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/skyborne/), [Icy Veins: Skyborne](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/all-skyborne-official-info-customization-druid-forms-zones-classes-abilities-and-more/)).

- Forever's map numbering isn't published. The addon uses Classic Era's map IDs: Stormwind 1453, Orgrimmar 1454, Ironforge 1455, Thunder Bluff 1456, Darnassus 1457, Undercity 1458 (retail's would be 84, 85, 87, 88, 89, 90) (#34). **Confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).
- The Skyborne have one hub, Valanaar on Zephras Isle, shared by High Order and Windshaper; guides disagree on whether it's a capital. A skyship runs from it to Dalaran, which the owner confirms is an Alliance city on Forever. Both count as capitals ([owner's decision on #34](https://github.com/CKozanecki/FriendlyForever/issues/34#issuecomment-6484459000)). Their map IDs are not checked yet: the owner can't reach them (Valanaar isn't a traditional capital; Dalaran needs an Alliance character). See #207.
- Whether entering a city fires `ZONE_CHANGED_NEW_AREA` (#34): **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).

## Buffs

From #68 (beta build 70425, mid-October 2026; [Paladin deep dive](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/blue-tracker/news/eu/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-paladin-world-of-warcraft-blizzard-24302099), [Blessing of Salvation (Forever)](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1038/blessing-of-salvation), [paladin talent calculator](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/talent-calc/paladin), [forg.gg build 70425](https://forg.gg/en/forever/tips/build-70425)). These are beta sources; the role-API check below was **confirmed in game**; the Salvation, Mass Salvation and Revoke checks no longer apply, because Blizzard removed those spells (launch, Nov 4).

- **Blessings last 1 hour,** up from 5 or 15 minutes (#68).
- **Salvation isn't a Blessing** (removed by Blizzard right before launch, tank threat was buffed instead: owner, 2026-11-08; #208 handles the code): a member can wear your Salvation and one of your Blessings. Salvation (spell 1038) cuts the target's threat by 30% for 1 hour, on a party or raid member; it's learned at level 26 (#68).
- **Mass Salvation** (removed by Blizzard right before launch: owner, 2026-11-08) (formerly Greater Blessing of Salvation) covers every member of the target's class in the group; learned at level 60, it uses a Symbol of Kings. The addon uses Classic's ID, 25895 (#68).
- **Revoke** is new at level 26: it takes Salvation or Mass Salvation off a party or raid member (instant, 40 yards, no cooldown). Blizzard removed Revoke right before launch (owner, 2026-11-08); the code is #208's. Its spell ID isn't recorded here, so the addon finds it by its English name, and doesn't offer it in other languages (#68, #74).
- Which role APIs exist (`UnitGroupRolesAssigned` or `GetPartyAssignment("MAINTANK", unit)`) (#68): **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).
- The buff spell IDs in `BuffData.lua` are Classic Era's (1.15); Forever's may differ. Source: as built for #27. Classic IDs are hand-kept today; #209 will look Forever's up from the game's spell API (owner, Nov 8).
- **Unending Breath** (warlock, 5697) has no group version. Spell 131 is the **shaman's** Water Breathing, which also lets the target breathe underwater, so `BuffData` lists it as a blocker (wearing it counts as having Unending Breath), not as a group version a warlock could cast. Source: [#135](https://github.com/CKozanecki/FriendlyForever/issues/135) ([Unending Breath](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Unending_Breath), [Water Breathing](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Water_Breathing), [classicdb 5697](https://classicdb.ch/?spell=5697), [classicdb 131](https://classicdb.ch/?spell=131)); Classic sources, as wowhead.com couldn't be reached. Both IDs, whether shamans have Water Breathing on Forever, and whether Forever adds a group water-breathing spell (it has an underwater dungeon; [owner on #135](https://github.com/CKozanecki/FriendlyForever/issues/135#issuecomment-6505287339)): confirmed in game (launch, Nov 4).
- **Water Breathing** (shaman, 131) is a buff the addon offers since #136: on Classic it's one rank, learned at level 22, lasting 10 minutes, at 30 yards, with a Shiny Fish Scales reagent. Source: [#136](https://github.com/CKozanecki/FriendlyForever/issues/136) ([Water Breathing](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Water_Breathing), [WoWClassicDB 131](https://wowclassicdb.com/tbc/spell/131), [Vanilla WoW Wiki](https://vanilla-wow-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Breathing)); Classic sources, found by web search, as wowhead.com couldn't be reached. Its ID, ranks and reagent on Forever: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4) (#136).
- **Near water** (#136): the addon reads `IsSwimming()`, `IsSubmerged()` and the breath bar (mirror timer `"BREATH"`, `GetMirrorTimerInfo`, `MIRROR_TIMER_START` and `MIRROR_TIMER_STOP`), which the modern API has; there's no call for whether another player is swimming. Source: as built for #136 ([IsSubmerged](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_IsSubmerged), [GetMirrorTimerInfo](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_GetMirrorTimerInfo)). Whether Forever has them and how they answer: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).
- **Underwater dungeons** (#136): **The Drowned City** is a new Forever dungeon, a sunken troll city off Stranglethorn Vale for levels 35 to 40 (some sources say 45), with flooded and underwater sections. Source: [#136](https://github.com/CKozanecki/FriendlyForever/issues/136) ([Warcraft Tavern preview](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/the-drowned-city-dungeon-preview-in-wow-forever/), [Mobalytics guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/dungeons/the-drowned-city-guide)), by web search. Its instance ID isn't published, so the addon matches its English name; Blackfathom Deeps uses Classic's instance ID, 48. Both IDs are not checked yet: the owner's character is too low a level to enter. See #207.
- Unit power is secret for strangers, so the addon decides who uses mana by class. Source: as built for #27 (`BuffData.MANA_CLASSES`); the owner confirms who has mana is consistent per class (Nov 8).
- Buff back remembers every buff of yours the game says reached a player, cast by hand too (#37). It relies on `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SENT` firing for every cast of yours with the target's name and a `castGUID`, and on `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED` giving the same `castGUID`, as in the retail API ([UNIT_SPELLCAST_SENT](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/UNIT_SPELLCAST_SENT)). Whether the name is the first name alone, or "First Surname", and whether a refreshed buff arrives as a new aura (`addedAuras`): **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).
- A thank-you waits at least 2 seconds after the last buff either way (`Exchange.SETTLE`, #36), assuming buffs are instant with a 1.5-second global cooldown, as in Classic. Source: [Mark of the Wild, classic data](https://wowclassicdb.com/tbc/spell/26990); a Forever beta writeup gives shaman totems a 1.0-second one ([finalboss.io](https://finalboss.io/world-of-warcraft-forever-how-to-prepare-a-shaman-build-beta)), and nothing was found for buffs. The assumption: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4).
- **Top-up timing** (#42) reads a group member's buff times from `C_UnitAuras.GetAuraDataByIndex`: `expirationTime` (on the `GetTime()` clock; 0 for a buff that never runs out) and `duration`, as in the retail API ([AuraData](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Struct_AuraData)). Under Midnight's rules some aura durations are hidden from addons, so the addon reads a secret, missing or junk time as "not running low". Whether Forever shows group members' buff times, and whether they're secret in combat or instances: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4). Blessings last an hour (above), so a Blessing is topped up only in its last minutes.
- Whether group members' aura caster (`sourceUnit`) is readable, whether `C_Spell.IsSpellInRange` or `IsSpellInRange` answer for them, and the exact "Out of range" and line-of-sight error texts: **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4) (from #30).

## Battlegrounds

From #71 ([research comment](https://github.com/CKozanecki/FriendlyForever/issues/71#issuecomment-6482177856); the [Forever beta development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696) don't mention battlegrounds).

- In a battleground, `IsInInstance()` says `"pvp"`. Forever may not use the instance group category for battlegrounds, so the addon checks the zone type too (#71).
- How Forever shows the preparation phase is unknown: the Preparation aura (Classic's 44521), `START_TIMER` with `TIMER_TYPE_PVP` (1) or `Enum.StartTimerType.PvPBeginTimer` (0), both or neither. `GetBattlefieldWinner()` is nil until the end. Whether a resurrection gives a Preparation aura again is unknown too (#71). All **confirmed in game** (launch, Nov 4) (#71).
