- **Weapon buffs (experimental, off by default):** a new options page, Weapon buffs, lets you choose a stone, oil, poison or shaman imbue for each hand. In buff mode, once your favours, your group and your self-buffs are done, the button offers it for a hand with nothing on it, the best one you carry, and one press puts it on. A weapon with its own Use is left for you. In a druid's form or Ghost Wolf it's offered too, and a press may drop you out of the form, as doing it by hand would (#45)
