- **Buff mode comes back when your buffs are missing:** in any group, once you're out of combat, buff mode turns itself on again when a member close by is missing one of your buffs, because it was never cast or has fully faded (not merely running low). That happens as a fight ends, or the moment a buff fades. It turns off once everyone has your buffs, or at the next pull, and never turns on in an arena or battleground. (#272)
