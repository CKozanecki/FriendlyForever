- **Buff mode comes back when your buffs are missing:** in any group, once you're out of combat, buff mode turns itself on again when a member close by (you included) is missing one of your buffs, because it was never cast or has fully faded (not merely running low). That happens as a fight ends, or the moment a buff fades. It turns off once everyone has your buffs, at the next pull, or after the usual timeout, and never turns on in an arena or battleground. Its switch, "when someone's missing my buffs", is on the Buff Back tab, on by default. (#272)
