- **Buff mode recasts over strangers' buffs:** in a group, buff mode now offers your buff to a member who has the same buff from someone outside the group (or from a caster the game doesn't name), so everyone's buffs are yours and on your timers. A buff a group member put up is left alone. On entering a dungeon or raid with your group, your own earlier buffs are offered once more as well, until the first pull, so the timers line up. It never casts over a stronger rank than you know, still keeps one Blessing of yours on each player, and in a raid follows the raid buff split. A buff whose caster the game won't show is never cast over, and that member doesn't count as buffed. Nothing casts without your press. (#256)
