- **Buff mode recasts over strangers' buffs, and whoever buffs first in a dungeon:** in a group, buff mode now offers your buff to a member who has the same buff from someone outside the group (or from a caster the game doesn't name), so everyone's buffs are yours and on your timers. A buff another group member put up is left alone until it's running low, when top-up renews it, and a group version isn't offered while it would replace one. On entering a dungeon or raid with your group, a buff (yours or another member's) put up more than 5 minutes before you entered is given once more, until the first pull, so the timers line up; one put up since then is left alone, and the button shows "Already done by Ann" in red for 2 seconds, empty, before the next buff you need. It never casts over a stronger rank than you know, still keeps one Blessing of yours on each player, and in a raid follows the raid buff split. A buff whose caster or cast time the game won't show is never cast over. Nothing casts without your press. (#256)
