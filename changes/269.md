- **Buff back renews a worn buff:** when someone you owe already wears your buff but it is partly worn off, the button offers it again instead of skipping to your next buff. A new setting on the Buff Back tab, "Renew my buff when theirs has under N% left" (default 70%, 0 = never), sets when; a stronger rank than yours or a group version is never cast over, and the debug log says when a buff is skipped because they wear it, with its time left.
