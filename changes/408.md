- **Buffs have a tab of their own.** The new Buffs tab holds your buff order (with each buff's icon), Never offer and the buff options. For each buff it says who gets it in each place: strangers who buff you (Buff Back), Party, Raid and PvP (battlegrounds and arenas). The choices are by role (Tanks, Healers, Damage), class or Everyone, with Always and Never names, or Off. In PvP every buff starts off. The first time, choose **Keep my settings** or **Use the new defaults**; until you do, nothing changes. (#408)
