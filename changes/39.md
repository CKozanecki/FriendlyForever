- **Buffs for passers-by:** with "Also offer my buffs to passers-by" on (off by default), buff mode offers your buffs to players nearby who aren't in your group and are missing one, or whose copy is half gone, after everyone else, one press each. Never in a dungeon, raid, battleground, arena or capital city, never to anyone flagged for PvP while you aren't, and nothing is said to them. Buff mode also turns on by itself when you stand by a campfire (you can switch that off). (#39)
