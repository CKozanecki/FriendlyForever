- **Each character has its own buff settings:** weapon buffs, buff mode's key, "Offer my self-buffs", the buff order with "Never offer", and who gets each buff are now each character's, so a priest and a druid no longer share them. Each character you had starts with a copy of the settings you had; a character made from now on starts with them unset (weapon buffs off). A character on a shared profile uses that profile's, and every other character switches back to its own at login. The walkthrough's new step names the pages these settings are on (#508)
