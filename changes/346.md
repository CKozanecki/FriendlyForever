- **Buff mode double-checks whether you're in a raid before it calls everyone buffed:** the game's answer must agree with the group it shows (in a raid, one of its slots is you; in a party, there are no raid slots), as "everyone is inside" already does. If they disagree, buff mode treats it like an answer it can't read: it keeps going, offers single buffs only (no group versions), and never ends as done on your own subgroup alone while another subgroup still lacks your buffs.
