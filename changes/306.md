- **A PvP-flagged group member gets your buffs once the whole group is inside a dungeon or raid:** buff mode never buffed a member flagged for PvP unless you were flagged too, even in a dungeon. Now, inside a dungeon or raid, once every member of your group is in there with you, a flagged member is offered single buffs and is covered by group versions and Greater Blessings like anyone else, since nobody can attack you there. Everywhere else (the open world, cities, battlegrounds, arenas), and while anyone is still outside, offline or can't be read, a flagged member is never buffed unless you're flagged too, as before.
