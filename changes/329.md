- **Buff mode buffs you when you're solo.** Out of a group, buff mode now offers your own Fortitude, Arcane Intellect, Mark of the Wild, Blessing and the like when you're missing them or they're running low, on you, one per press, after any favours and before your self-buffs. It follows your buff order, Never offer, who gets each buff and the top-up time, and offers single buffs only. Someone else's copy on you counts until it runs low. "Offer my own buffs when I'm not in a group" on the Buff Back tab (on by default) switches it off. (#329)
