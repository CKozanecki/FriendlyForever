- **Group versions never cover a group member the game won't show:** when the game wouldn't say whether a group slot held anyone, that slot was left out as empty, so Arcane Brilliance, a Prayer, Gift of the Wild or a Greater Blessing could still be offered and cover whoever was in it unchecked: a member flagged for PvP (flagging you), one your rules leave out, or one wearing another member's buff. Now no group version is offered while a slot can't be read: the button says "can't read a group member: buffing one at a time", and the members who can be read get the single buff. (#302)
