- **Buff mode isn't ended by a group member the game won't show:** when the game wouldn't say whether a group slot held anyone, that slot was passed over as empty, so "everyone has your buffs" could turn buff mode off with nobody's buffs read. Such a member now counts as one who can't be read: the button says "a group member can't be read", offers them nothing, and buff mode stays on until they can be read or the timeout ends it. (#259)
