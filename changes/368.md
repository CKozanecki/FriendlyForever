- **"My Blessings in this raid" is no longer forgotten by one bad answer from the game:** if the game, for one moment in a raid, said you weren't in one (or gave no readable answer), the Blessings you'd left to another paladin came back into buff mode, so both of you gave Kings, and nothing said the choice was gone. The choice is now forgotten only when the game says you're out of the raid and the raid's slots agree; `/friendly blessings all` stops choosing at any time. Telling a tank in a raid follows the same rule: while the game won't say whether you're in a raid, a member whose Main Tank assignment can't be read may still be the tank, so a buff you keep off tanks isn't given to them.
