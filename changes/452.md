- **Scrolling no longer knocks a druid out of a form.** In cat, bear, travel or any other form, buff mode's key doesn't cast your druid buffs: the button still shows the buff, says "click to buff (you're in a form)", and a click on it casts (the game takes you out of the form, as before). A new setting on the Buffs tab, "Fast-cast buffs in form" (off by default), lets the key cast in a form too. Weapon buffs work with the key as before. (#452)
