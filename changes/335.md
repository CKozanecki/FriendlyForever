- **"Everyone is inside" double-checks whether you're in a raid:** before a PvP-flagged member is buffed in a dungeon or raid, or buff mode stops waiting there, the game's answer to whether you're in a raid must agree with the group it shows (in a raid, one of its slots is you; in a party, there are no raid slots). If the two disagree, or either can't be read, nobody counts as inside: a flagged member isn't buffed and buff mode keeps waiting, the safe side. (#335)
