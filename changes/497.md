- **Raid leader's buff assignments (experimental):** the raid leader and raid assistants can set who gives which buff to which groups, in `/friendly raid` (for example Fortitude: "Ann Vale 1-4, Bob Lake 5-8"). Copies in the raid then offer an assigned buff only to the groups it's assigned for; buffs nobody is assigned keep the raid buff split, and an assignment to someone offline, out of the raid, without the addon or without that buff falls back to the split for that buff. Each copy checks the sender's rank in its own raid roster on every message, and an assignment can only narrow what your copy offers, never cast. Leaving the raid forgets them; a new raid leader clears them. Tried in tests only so far: tell us how it goes in a raid.
